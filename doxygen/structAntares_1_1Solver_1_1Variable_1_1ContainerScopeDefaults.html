Detailed Description

Empty default implementations of the per-scope lifecycle hooks.

Top-level container wrappers (SetsOfAreas, ...) are not Variable::IVariable subclasses, so unlike leaf variables they have no base supplying empty defaults for the lifecycle hooks they do not use. They would otherwise have to spell out a no-op version of every such hook. Deriving from this struct provides those no-ops; a wrapper only redefines the hooks it actually needs.
