Detailed Description

template<class Inner>
class Antares::Solver::Variable::Container::List< Inner >

Top-level container for all output variables.

Owns the inner aggregator (a JoinAll<…> of scope wrappers) by composition and forwards every hook to it. Adds dynamic-aggregation bookkeeping that isn't part of the inner aggregator's responsibilities.
